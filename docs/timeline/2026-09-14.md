# Learning Timeline — 2026-09-14

- [Codeforces 120D](../../src/codeforces/set0/set1/set120/set120/d/): If the field total is not `A+B+C` there is no split; otherwise every permutation of `(A,B,C)` is tried as left/mid/right targets on both the row-sum and column-sum arrays, collecting prefixes equal to the left target and suffixes equal to the right target that stay strictly after those prefixes, so the middle is forced.
- [Codeforces 2258B2](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2258/b2/): Suffix counts `freq[i]` = number of carrots with size `≥ i`; once `2^k > m` every unit can be isolated so the answer is the total length, otherwise each candidate piece length `x` is scored by how many original carrots reach `x, 2x, …` up to a `2^k`-piece cap.
- [Codeforces 2259D](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2259/d/): Impossible iff `0` appears once; otherwise one `0` goes to `A`, the remaining zeros to `B`, and every nonzero to `C` (or all `C` when there are no zeros), giving `mex(A)=mex(B)` and `mex(C)=0`.
