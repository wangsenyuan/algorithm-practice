# Learning Timeline — 2026-07-27

- [Codeforces 2189D1](../../src/codeforces/set2/set21/set218/set2189/d1/): Building the permutation by inserting `0..n-1` in order, MEX `k` exists exactly when `k` sits outside the span of smaller values, so `f(s)` is a product of factors `2` or `k-1`; successive `gcd` updates strip `c`'s primes to test divisibility without big integers.
- [Codeforces 2189D2](../../src/codeforces/set2/set21/set218/set2189/d2/): Each `?` first takes the locally smaller factor to minimize the product; if that product is still divisible by `c`, only flipping even positions from factor `2` to odd `k-1` can drop the 2-adic valuation enough, and the fewest such flips is `v2(B)-v2(c)+1`.
- [Codeforces 2199C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set21/set219/set2199/c/): A valid minimum-width 2-row field exists iff `k ≢ 2,4 (mod 5)`; mines spaced three apart in remainder-specific blocks make each mine contribute a clean pack of threatened empties without double-counting.
- [Codeforces 2227D](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set222/set2227/d/): Expanding while ends stay equal from the midpoint between the two zeros and from each zero alone tracks a running MEX over the forced palindrome contents, and the maximum among those expansions is the answer.
- [Codeforces 2241E](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set224/set2241/e/): In a triplet the three path-products multiply so only the unique median vertex has odd exponent; counting component-size pairs and triples around each square-valued vertex enumerates every good median once.
