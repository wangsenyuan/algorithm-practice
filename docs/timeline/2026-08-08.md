- [Codeforces 135B](../../src/codeforces/set0/set1/set130/set135/b/): Enumerate candidate squares by taking an edge and rotating it 90° to recover the other two corners from a point map, then permute the leftover four points until every consecutive corner is a right angle.

- [Codeforces 2249C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set224/set2249/c/): A circular start is good iff every prefix has ≤2 consecutive-integer blocks; maintain prefix block counts under rotation with a lazy range-add / range-max segment tree on left-endpoint contributions.
