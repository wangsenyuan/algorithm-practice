# Learning Timeline — 2026-08-21

- [Codeforces 1551B2](../../src/codeforces/set1/set15/set155/set1551/b2/): Each value keeps at most `k` paint slots; cycling colors `1..k` over the first `⌊s/k⌋·k` of those indices equalizes color counts, and a value’s contiguous block is shorter than one period so it never repeats a color.
- [Codeforces 2200G](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set220/set2200/g/): Fold `-`/`/` into field `+`/`×`; the initial `x` is multiplied by every factor, while additions share one expected trailing product from a subset-size product DP weighted by `k!(m-k)!/(m+1)!`.
- [Codeforces 2215A](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set221/set2215/a/): Each index ends as `a%p` or `(a%q)%p`; a length-`k` monochromatic seed makes the rest independent, so prefix sums score a forced window plus unconstrained `min` on every other cell.
- [Codeforces 2245C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set224/set2245/c/): Final MEX is always `n`, so realize `k⊕n` as holes after `0` (one value, or high-bit `w` plus remainder); a bit `w≥n` cannot appear as a prefix MEX.
- [Codeforces 2252C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2252/c/): Emptying a level costs `m`; otherwise scan top-down and, with a sum/count segment tree on remaining lower pieces, take the fewest largest values whose sum meets `v_i`, then drop the row.
- [Codeforces 2254F](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2254/f/): With even `n`, XORing everyone except `i` by `a_i` preserves the multiset `{a_j ⊕ (⊕a)} ∪ {⊕a}`; sort that signature on both arrays and compare.
