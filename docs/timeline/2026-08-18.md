# Learning Timeline — 2026-08-18

- [Codeforces 2127E](../../src/codeforces/set2/set21/set212/set2127/e/): Forced cuties are LCAs of consecutive same-color vertices in DFS order; paint an uncolored LCA with its single forcing color, pay every precolored conflict or multi-forced LCA, then copy ancestor colors down the tree.
- [Codeforces 2205C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set220/set2205/c/): Each blog collapses to last-occurrence order (reverse + unique); repeatedly append the remaining block that minimizes the `a+b` vs `b+a` concatenation and freeze those users.
- [Codeforces 2252B](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2252/b/): Scan both alternating targets `01…` and `10…`, keep the longest matched prefix whose leftover `0`/`1` counts differ by ≤1, and delete the rest (`n` minus that keep, or `-1`).
- [Codeforces 2252D](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2252/d/): The replace op swaps adjacent same-parity differences, so sort each contiguous same-parity difference block ascending and rebuild the array from `a_1`.
- [Codeforces 2253C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2253/c/): Cap row-only / column-only values at `n` / `m`, then maximize the sum of at most `n+m-1` distinct entries by taking the largest exclusives and filling from shared values.
- [Codeforces 2254E](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set225/set2254/e/): `a` is the prefix sums of a permutation of `b`; greedily take the smallest remaining value that keeps the prefix ≥ 1 (max-segtree on sorted `b`), which is lex-optimal and fails exactly when reconstruction is impossible.
