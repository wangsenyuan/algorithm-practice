# Learning Timeline — 2026-10-27

- [Codeforces 219B](../../src/codeforces/set0/set2/set210/set219/b/): Try each trailing-nine length from short to long, fill the largest prefix that keeps the price at most `p`, and keep the price when the discount is at most `d`, so a longer feasible run replaces a shorter one.
- [Codeforces 357B](../../src/codeforces/set0/set3/set350/set357/b/): Each dance adds at most one dancer who already has a color. A fresh triple is `1, 2, 3`; otherwise the known color `c` stays, the next dancer takes `c + 1` with `3` wrapping to `1`, and the last dancer takes `6 - c - next`.
- [Codeforces 358A](../../src/codeforces/set0/set3/set350/set358/a/): Upper semicircles cross exactly when the diameters properly overlap. On compressed endpoints, a range-maximum tree stores the farthest right at each left index and a range-minimum tree stores the farthest left at each right index, so a new chord crosses an older one when an interior endpoint continues outside the open interval.
- [Codeforces 2137D](../../src/codeforces/set2/set21/set213/set2137/d/): Positions that claim frequency `v` must split into groups of size exactly `v`, each group receiving one fresh value. The count of those positions must be divisible by `v`, and `v` itself cannot exceed `n`.
- [Codeforces 2276C](../../src/codeforces/set2/set22/set227/set2276/c/): A subarray sum is divisible by `k` when the prefix residues at its ends match. A prefix enters the frequency map only after `k` later elements, so every hit is a subarray longer than `k`.
